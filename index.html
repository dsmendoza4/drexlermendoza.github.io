ABOUT ME

Hi! I'm Drexler, a Digital Design and Art student at the University of the Philippines Open University. I'm interested in digital illustration, multimedia, animation, and other forms of visual art. I've always enjoyed creating things and exploring different ways to express ideas visually. I mainly work with digital illustration, but I've also been learning more about video editing, animation, graphic design, and other multimedia tools as I continue my studies. Outside of school, I enjoy playing games, listening to music, watching anime and tokusatsu, and exploring different kinds of art and media. I'm still figuring out where my interests will take me, but I'm always interested in learning something new and improving my skills along the way.
